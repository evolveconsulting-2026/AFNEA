Who we are

Education by and for our community

The Attawapiskat First Nation Education Authority (AFNEA) leads education in Attawapiskat, on the west coast of James Bay. We operate Kattawapiskak Elementary School and Vezina Secondary School, and support learners of every age through independent learning, community programs and post-secondary support.

We believe that strong learners grow from strong roots: our language, our families, our Elders and our land.

What guides us

  • Cree language and culture in everyday learning
  • Student safety, health and wellbeing
  • Families and Elders as partners
  • High expectations and support for every learner
  • Learning for life, at every age
