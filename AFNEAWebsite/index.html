Attawapiskat First Nation Education Authority

The Attawapiskat First Nation Education Authority (AFNEA) leads education for our community on the west coast of James Bay.

We are home to Kattawapiskak Elementary School and Vezina Secondary School, and we support learners beyond the classroom through independent learning, community programs and help with college and university.

Our work is guided by the community. We want learning that is rooted in Cree language, culture and the land, and that prepares every student to succeed wherever their path leads.
