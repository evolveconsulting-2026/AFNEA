About the school

Learning that prepares you for what's next

Vezina Secondary School serves students in Grades 9 to 12 in Attawapiskat.

Students work toward their Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) at a school that brings the provincial curriculum together with Cree language, culture and learning on the land. Small classes and caring staff help every student find their strengths and plan for college, university, training or work.

At Vezina you can

  • Earn Ontario high school credits toward your diploma
  • Study Cree language and culture
  • Take part in land-based learning with Elders and knowledge keepers
  • Get guidance on post-secondary applications, scholarships and careers
  • Join sports, clubs and student leadership

New student or returning to school? Contact the Board Office and we'll connect you with the school team. See also Beyond the School.
