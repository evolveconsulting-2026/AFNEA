About the program

Learn at your own pace

The Independent Learning System gives learners a flexible way to keep moving forward when a regular classroom timetable doesn't fit.

Courses are self-paced and supported by AFNEA staff, so youth and adults can earn credits, upgrade their marks or finish their high school diploma, whether they are returning to school, working, caring for family or catching up.

How it works

  • Choose courses with help from a counsellor
  • Work through lessons online, at home or at school
  • Check in regularly with a teacher for help and feedback
  • Earn credits toward your Ontario Secondary School Diploma
