About the program

Learning doesn't stop at graduation

Community Learning offers programs for adults and families who want to build new skills, earn certificates or reconnect with learning.

Programs run in person in Attawapiskat and online, and are planned with the community so they fit local jobs, needs and interests.

Programs may include

  • Literacy, upgrading and preparation for further education
  • Workplace safety and skills certificates
  • Job readiness and career planning
  • Cree language and cultural programs for all ages
  • Workshops for parents and families

Watch for upcoming sessions in the Newsletter, or contact the Board Office to ask about programs.
